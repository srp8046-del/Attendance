Reports & Rules

Live calculation rules.

BIO · Employee-wise shift rule

For every employee, use their own Employee Master SHIFT IN.

IN ≤ employee Shift In → PRESENT if working ≥ 07:30.

After Shift In and within 60 minutes → LATE if working ≥ 07:30.

More than 60 minutes late → HALFDAY when working ≥ 04:00.

Working < 04:00 → ABSENT.

Working < 07:30 → HALFDAY even when punch is on time.

TOS

TOS = LOGIN − LUNCH − TEA − TRAINING.

<04:00 ABSENT · 04:00–06:59 HALFDAY · 07:00–07:59 LATE · ≥08:00 PRESENT.

Tele Sales Executive

BIO and TOS are both calculated.

Final = worse status.

ABSENT > HALFDAY > LATE > PRESENT.

Other designations

TOS ignored.

Final = BIO only.
