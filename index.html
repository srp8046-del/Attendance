Reports & Rules

Live calculation rules used by the attendance engine.

BIO · Employee-wise shift

Each employee uses their own SHIFT IN from Employee Master.

IN ≤ Shift In = PRESENT when working ≥ 07:30.

Shift In < IN ≤ Shift In + 60 min = LATE when working ≥ 07:30.

IN > Shift In + 60 min = HALFDAY when working ≥ 04:00.

Working < 04:00 = ABSENT.

Working < 07:30 = HALFDAY, even when the punch is on time.

TOS

TOS = LOGIN − LUNCH − TEA − TRAINING.

<04:00 ABSENT · 04:00–06:59 HALFDAY · 07:00–07:59 LATE · ≥08:00 PRESENT.

Tele Sales Executive

Final = worse status.

ABSENT > HALFDAY > LATE > PRESENT.

Other designations

TOS ignored.

Final = BIO only.
