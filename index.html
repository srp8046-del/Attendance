Reports & Rules

Live calculation rules used by the engine.

BIO shift rule

Shift 09:30 → up to 09:30 = PRESENT.

09:31–10:30 = LATE.

After 10:30 = HALFDAY when working ≥ 4:00.

Working < 4:00 = ABSENT.

Any working time < 7:30 = HALFDAY even if the punch is otherwise on time.

TOS rule

TOS = LOGIN − LUNCH − TEA − TRAINING.

< 04:00 = ABSENT · 04:00–06:59 = HALFDAY · 07:00–07:59 = LATE · ≥ 08:00 = PRESENT.

Tele Sales Executive

BIO and TOS are both calculated.

Final = worse status of BIO and TOS.

BIO NOT PUNCHED remains NOT PUNCHED.

Other designations

TOS is ignored.

Final = BIO status only.
