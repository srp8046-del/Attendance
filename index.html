Reports & Rules

Live calculation rules.

BIO shift rule

IN ≤ Shift IN → PRESENT.

After Shift IN and within 60 minutes → LATE.

More than 60 minutes late + working ≥ 04:00 → HALFDAY.

Working < 04:00 → ABSENT.

Working < 07:30 → HALFDAY regardless of punch time.

TOS rule

TOS = LOGIN − LUNCH − TEA − TRAINING.

< 04:00 = ABSENT · 04:00–06:59 = HALFDAY · 07:00–07:59 = LATE · ≥ 08:00 = PRESENT.

Tele Sales Executive

BIO and TOS are both calculated.

Final = worse of BIO and TOS.

Severity: ABSENT > HALFDAY > LATE > PRESENT.

Other designations

TOS is ignored.

Final = BIO only.
