Reports & Rules

BIO · Employee-wise shift

Each employee uses their own SHIFT IN from Employee Master.

Missing IN or OUT = NP.

IN ≤ Shift In = P when working ≥ 07:30.

Shift In < IN ≤ Shift In + 60 min = LC when working ≥ 07:30.

IN > Shift In + 60 min = HD when working ≥ 04:00.

Working < 04:00 = A.

TOS

TOS Hours = LOGIN − LUNCH − TEA − TRAINING.

<04:00 A · 04:00–06:59 HD · 07:00–07:59 LC · ≥08:00 P.

Hours display as HH:MM:SS.
